Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026  ·  AgentFlow, operated by Sudip Dolai

This Privacy Policy explains how AgentFlow ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Sudip Dolai, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform at getagentflow.co.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you register
  • Business information: WhatsApp Business number, email address used for lead follow-up
  • Lead data: Contact details of your property leads (name, phone number, email, property interest)
  • Usage data: How you interact with AgentFlow, features used, and performance metrics
  • Communication data: Messages sent and received through our automated follow-up system
  • Payment information: Billing details processed securely through our payment provider

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the AgentFlow platform and its features
  • Send automated WhatsApp and email follow-up messages on your behalf to your leads
  • Generate AI-powered property listings and reports
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send you service updates, security alerts, and support messages
  • Improve and develop our platform based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. WhatsApp & Meta Data

When you connect your WhatsApp Business number to AgentFlow via Meta's WhatsApp Business API:

  • We store your WhatsApp Business Account ID and Phone Number ID
  • We access your WhatsApp messages solely to provide automated follow-up services
  • We do not read, store, or share your personal WhatsApp conversations outside of the AgentFlow platform
  • Message content is used only to track lead engagement and trigger follow-up sequences
  • We comply fully with Meta's Platform Terms and WhatsApp Business Policy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: To send messages via WhatsApp Business API
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): To generate automated message content and property listings
  • Payment processors: To handle subscription billing securely
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your AgentFlow account is active. If you cancel your account:

  • Your account data is deleted within 30 days of cancellation
  • Lead data and message history are permanently deleted within 30 days
  • You may request immediate deletion by contacting us

6. Your Rights (GDPR — UK & EU Users)

If you are based in the UK or European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to how we process your data
  • Restriction: Request we limit processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@getagentflow.co.

7. Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement the following measures:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption)
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • We regularly review our security practices

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.

9. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites such as Bayut, Property Finder, Rightmove, and Zoopla. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

AgentFlow is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify you by email if the changes are significant.
